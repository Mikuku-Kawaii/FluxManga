FluxManga modifications:
- Reuse the gallery core with an explicit site parameter. Separate EX settings,
  source identity, user-entered credentials, cache and progress. No automatic EH
  credential copying, account merging, content fallback or shared cookie jar.
- Require forum identity plus readable EX index and EX favorites before saving
  EX credentials. Saved identity is not a claim of continuing access. Recognize
  denied, empty, challenge and Sad Panda responses without treating EH as EX.
- Send igneous only to EX; forum requests use identity cookies only. Restrict
  protected image cookies to same-site fullimg and EX /t/ thumbnails; external
  image requests and redirects strip credentials. HTTPS only; no quota bypass.
- Preserve returned favorite categories/cursors and explicit current-batch
  import. Report same-gallery overlap with EH records without merging progress.
- Reuse existing encrypted image decoding, reader modes, statistics, library,
  navigation and lifecycle. No new runtime, database or whole upstream host.
